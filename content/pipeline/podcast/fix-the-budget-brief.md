# Fix The Budget! — Podcast Brief

**Runtime:** ~20 minutes
**Tenet:** AI + Clarity
**Format:** Solo narration with live demo description

---

## Cold Open (30 seconds)

> "Everyone's an armchair Chancellor — until they actually try it. I built a simulator that lets you run the UK's finances. Within 30 seconds, I'd triggered a bond market panic. Here's what that taught me about democracy, empathy, and why we're all terrible at fiscal policy."

---

## Segment 1: The Problem (4 min)

**Setup:** Why do people complain about government budgets without understanding them?

Key points:
- The micro-macro fallacy — household budget logic doesn't work for sovereign currency issuers
- Abstract numbers create abstract anger — £100bn is meaningless
- The empathy gap — cuts are numbers until they close your local hospital

**Quote:** "Every armchair Chancellor has an opinion. None of them have opened the Red Box."

---

## Segment 2: The Simulation (8 min)

**Deep Dive:** Walking through the simulator mechanic by mechanic.

1. **The Red Box Onboarding**
   - Drama before data: "The previous government has fled. The Treasury is yours."
   - Making users feel weight before they touch controls

2. **The Sankey Flow**
   - Visual money flow from taxes to services
   - Making invisible infrastructure visible

3. **The Empathy Algorithms**
   - £1bn NHS cut = 45,000 more on waiting lists
   - Every number has a human translation

4. **The News Ticker**
   - AI-generated headlines that scream at you
   - "HOSPITALS REJOICE" then "MARKETS IN PANIC"
   - No right answers, only trade-offs

5. **The Bond Vigilantes**
   - Run deficit too high → borrowing costs spike
   - Real lesson from the 2022 Truss mini-budget

---

## Segment 3: The Lesson (5 min)

**Application:** What can listeners steal from this?

1. **Progressive Disclosure** — Don't overwhelm. Build mental models first.
2. **Narrative Volatility** — Abstract dashboards don't create urgency. Stories do.
3. **Empathy Algorithms** — Every business metric should have a human translation.
4. **Citizen Panels** — What if you let your customers "vote" on your roadmap?

**Quote:** "The closer you get people to the core of the business, the more invested they feel."

---

## Segment 4: The Bigger Picture (3 min)

**Outro:** Democracy's participation problem.

- When I was a journalist, I ran community news conferences
- Readers shaped our editorial agenda
- Same principle: participation creates investment
- What if government had a "GitHub for policy" where citizens could submit pull requests?

**Tease:** Coming up — we're building an Internal Communications simulator next. Stay tuned.
